Hi! I'm Emily Newman, a first-year PhD student in Robotics at CU Boulder. I received a BS in Computer Science and Robotics from Carnegie Mellon in 2019. Previously I worked at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, on various projects including robot simulation, spacecraft visualization, ground data systems for CADRE, and Curiosity Mars rover surface operations. I also worked at CMU's Software Engineering Institute, focusing on robot simulation for test and evaluation. My research interests include reliable multi-robot planning, and the fusion of multi-agent path finding methods with uncertainty-aware motion planning. I am co-advised by Dr. Nisar Ahmed. I also enjoy hiking, climbing, skiing, and playing volleyball, and am likely to be found on a mountain somewhere.
