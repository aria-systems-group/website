Hello! I am a Masters student in the Robotics Department, originally from Medina, Ohio. I earned my B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Notre Dame, where I also founded IrishSat, a student satellite club. Prior to graduate school, I worked as an Aerospace Engineer, focusing on rendezvous and proximity operations planning and logic development. My research interests center on creating motion planning algorithms that return low-cost trajectories for complicated systems in real-time.
