Hi! I'm Georgina, a PhD student in Aerospace Engineering at CU Boulder, working on autonomous systems and robotics for planetary exploration. I received my MSc in Aerospace Engineering, specializing in Space Systems, from ISAE-SUPAERO in Toulouse, France, and my BSc in Aerospace Engineering from UPC in Barcelona, Spain. Previously, I worked at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory on the aerodynamics of next-generation Mars helicopters and at PLD Space on the Miura 5 launch vehicle program. Along the way, I've also worked on life-support systems, analog space missions, and a few other projects involving autonomous systems, robotics, and planetary exploration. My research interests lie in making  robots more capable and reliable in uncertain and extreme environments. On a slightly different note, I'm also trying to bring shrimps to space :))
